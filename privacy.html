Privacy Policy

Missing Time for Jira. Last updated 18 October 2026.

Who we are

Missing Time is published on the Atlassian Marketplace by Christopher Dato, operating as an individual developer in Côte d'Ivoire. For any question about this policy or about data handled by the app, write to SUPPORT_EMAIL_TO_BE_SET.

The short version

The app sends no data anywhere. It runs entirely inside Atlassian's own infrastructure on the Forge platform. It makes no network calls to any server operated by us or by any third party, it contains no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and it never writes to your Jira data.

What the app reads from Jira

DataWhy
Worklogs: author account ID, date started, duration To calculate how much time each person logged on each day. This is the entire purpose of the app.
Users: account ID, display name, avatar URL, active status, account type To list the people a report covers, and to exclude bot and integration accounts which never owe a timesheet.
The signed-in user's own profile To confirm who is using the app.

The app requests the scopes read:jira-work, read:jira-user and storage:app. All three are read-only with respect to your Jira content; the app holds no permission to create, modify or delete anything in Jira.

What the app stores, and where

Storage uses Atlassian Forge's hosted key-value store. The data stays within the Atlassian infrastructure serving your site, under Atlassian's own security and data residency arrangements. We operate no database and hold no copy.

StoredContentsRetention
Settings Expected hours per working day, public holiday dates, and per-person overrides (hours per day, and whether a person is expected to log time at all), keyed by Atlassian account ID. Until changed or until the app is uninstalled.
Roster Account ID and display name of the people the report covers. Required because the scheduled scan runs without a signed-in user and cannot list Jira users on its own. Refreshed each time the app is opened. Removed on uninstall.
Weekly history One record per week: the week's dates, and for each person who logged less than expected, their account ID, display name and the shortfall in hours. 26 weeks maximum. Older weeks are deleted automatically.

No worklog is ever copied into this store. Hours shown on screen are read from Jira each time a report is run.

Who else sees it

Nobody. There are no sub-processors, no third-party services, no external APIs. We do not sell, rent or share any data, and we have no technical means of accessing a customer's stored data remotely.

Roles under data protection law

Where the GDPR or comparable law applies, the customer organisation installing the app is the data controller and we act as a data processor on their instructions. The Data Processing Addendum sets out the terms of that relationship.

The app processes information about employees' working time. Requests from individuals to access, correct or erase their data should be addressed to their own organisation, which controls the Jira site and can remove the data directly.

Deleting everything

Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored. Nothing is retained, archived or backed up by us, because we never held it.
