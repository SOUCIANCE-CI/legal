Missing Time for Jira

See who's behind on logging time, and by how much.

Missing Time reports on time logged in Jira and shows the people who logged less than expected, including the ones who logged nothing at all and therefore never appear in a conventional timesheet report.

It reads Jira's native worklogs. It keeps no parallel copy of them, which is why your JQL queries keep working and why leaving the app costs you nothing.
