Data Processing Addendum

Missing Time for Jira. Last updated 18 October 2026.

This addendum forms part of the End User Licence Agreement and applies where we process personal data on your behalf under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or comparable legislation.

1. Roles

You are the controller. We are the processor. You decide why and how personal data is processed; we process it only on your documented instructions, which for this app means operating the features you have enabled.

2. Subject matter and duration

Subject matter: reporting on time logged in your Jira site. Duration: for as long as the app is installed.

3. Categories of data subject

Users of your Jira site, typically your employees and contractors.

No special categories of personal data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR are processed. The app does not read issue content, comments, attachments or email addresses.

5. Sub-processors

None. The app performs no outbound network call to any system other than the Atlassian APIs of your own site. All processing and all storage take place within Atlassian's infrastructure, which you already engage directly as your own processor for Jira. We therefore engage no sub-processor and have no list to maintain.

6. International transfers

We initiate no transfer of personal data. Data remains within the Atlassian infrastructure serving your site, subject to Atlassian's own data residency arrangements, over which we exercise no control and to which we add nothing.

7. Security

The app runs on Atlassian Forge, which provides the hosting, isolation, encryption in transit and at rest, and access control. We hold no credentials to your site, operate no server, and have no remote access to stored data. Our own obligations are to request only the minimum scopes required, to store the minimum data required, and to delete it on uninstall.

8. Assisting you

Because all data is held in your own Jira site and in Forge storage attached to it, you can satisfy most data subject requests without us. Where you still need help, write to contact@souciance.com and we will assist within the limits of what the platform exposes to us.

9. Personal data breach

If we become aware of a breach affecting personal data processed by the app, we will notify you without undue delay with the information available to us.

10. Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. We retain no copy, since we never hold one.

11. Audit

On reasonable written request, we will provide the information needed to demonstrate compliance with this addendum. The app's source behaviour, the scopes it requests and the data it stores are described in the Privacy Policy.
